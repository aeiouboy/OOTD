## Key Insights for Women's Catalog

### Strengths
1. **Exceptional dress selection** (167 items) spanning budget to luxury — covers weekend, date night, office, and special events
2. **Strong blouse inventory** (114 items) — the most versatile women's occasion item, works for office, date, weekend
3. **Good brand tier spread** — can serve THB 159 budget shoppers to THB 38,900 luxury buyers
4. **Thai designer representation** — ASAVA and ASV bring local designer credibility for special occasions
5. **Trendy/Instagram brands** — Mardi Mercredi Actif, Sporty & Rich appeal to the 15-28 social media demographic

### Gaps to Address
1. **Blazers** (only 20 items) — critical gap for office and special event completeness
2. **Active/sportswear** (~8% coverage) — weakest occasion, needs Nike/Adidas expansion
3. **Accessories** — almost zero shoes, bags, jewelry — limits outfit completion
4. **Swimwear** — zero coverage despite travel/vacation being a top occasion
5. **Loungewear/athleisure** — growing category for WFH/weekend, currently underserved

### Recommended Priority Actions
1. **Tag all 1,247 women's products** with occasion scores (immediate)
2. **Create "Shop by Occasion" women's landing pages**: Weekend Brunch, Date Night, Office Ready, Event Glam
3. **Build outfit capsules** using cross-brand combinations (e.g., Espada blazer + Lacoste shirt + Amila trousers for office)
4. **Highlight the luxury tier** — Simkhai, Sandro, Maje as "Special Occasion" hero brands
5. **Expand blazers and activewear** in next catalog refresh
