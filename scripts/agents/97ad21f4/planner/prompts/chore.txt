## Issue Analysis
The current outfit generator is recommending outfits with 2 blazers (black and navy) in the same look. This is a styling error - an outfit should have maximum ONE item per category, especially for outerwear.

## Root Cause
The outfit-generator.ts and enhanced-outfit-generator.ts are not properly enforcing 'one item per category' rules, particularly for:
- Outerwear (blazers, jackets, coats, cardigans)
- Tops (should be 1 main top OR 1 top + 1 layering piece)
- Bottoms (only 1 bottom per outfit)

## Fix Requirements

### 1. Add Category Deduplication Logic
- Ensure only ONE blazer/jacket/coat per outfit
- Ensure only ONE bottom (pants/skirt/shorts) per outfit
- Allow layering: 1 base top + 1 layering piece (cardigan OR blazer, not both)

### 2. Implement Proper Outfit Composition Rules (Pinterest 2026)
Based on Pinterest analysis:

**Work Outfit Formula:**
- 1 fitted top (blouse/shirt) OR 1 base layer + 1 blazer
- 1 wide-leg/tailored trouser
- 1 footwear (heels/loafers)
- 1-2 accessories (bag, belt, jewelry)

**Casual Outfit Formula:**
- 1 top OR 1 base layer + 1 cardigan/sweater
- 1 bottom (jeans/pants)
- 1 footwear
- 1-2 accessories

### 3. Add Outfit Validation
- Before returning outfit, validate no duplicate categories
- If duplicates found, keep the best-scoring item and remove others
- Add logging for debugging outfit composition
