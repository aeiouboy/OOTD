# Summary: Thai Culture & Fashion Context
**Quick Reference Guide (1-page)**

## Overview
Thai cultural values in fashion and complete auspicious color system. Presentation matters culturally; dress shows respect and understanding of Thai traditions.

## Thai Fashion Values
- **Presentation matters**: Appearance judged; effort appreciated
- **Modesty valued**: Shoulders/knees covered in formal/temple settings
- **Social hierarchy**: Dress appropriately for status; brand awareness = status symbol
- **Color sensitivity**: Yellow = Royal (respect), Black/white = funerals, Festive colors for weddings

## Regional Differences
- **Bangkok**: Most fashion-forward, international influence OK, Western trends accepted
- **Chiang Mai/North**: Bohemian appreciated, more casual, cooler climate considerations
- **Phuket/South**: Beach casual standard, more revealing OK in resort areas
- **Isaan/Northeast**: Traditional dress values, conservative, functional over fashion
- **Deep South**: Muslim influence strong, must cover shoulders/knees, respect local culture

## Thai Fashion Etiquette
✅ **DO**: Remove shoes (homes/temples), dress up for shopping (better service!), cover temples, respect occasions
❌ **DON'T**: Wear all black to happy occasions, white to weddings, reveal at temples, be too casual in formal settings

## Thai Auspicious Colors (สีมงคลไทย)

### Birth Day Colors (Primary system)
- **Monday (Yellow)**: Wealth, abundance
- **Tuesday (Pink/Red)**: Love, power, courage
- **Wednesday Day (Green)**: Growth, wisdom
- **Wednesday Night (Gray/Dark)**: Mystery, protection
- **Thursday (Orange)**: Wisdom, prosperity, luck ⭐ BEST!
- **Friday (Blue)**: Love, happiness, beauty
- **Saturday (Purple/Black)**: Stability, slow but steady
- **Sunday (Red)**: Power, leadership, victory

### Specific Fortune Colors
- **Financial luck**: Gold, yellow, emerald, orange-gold
- **Love luck**: Pink (Tuesday), blue (Friday) ⭐ BEST
- **Career luck**: Orange (Thursday), green (Wed), red (Sunday) ⭐ Leaders
- **Health luck**: Green, blue pastels, orange
- **Windfall luck**: Orange-gold (Thursday) ⭐⭐⭐ LOTTERY!

### Kaala-Keenee (Time-Based Colors)
Same day = different colors by time:
- **Night (00:00-06:00)**: Rest/reflection colors
- **Morning (06:00-12:00)**: New beginning colors
- **Afternoon (12:00-18:00)**: Action/completion colors
- **Evening (18:00-00:00)**: Relationship colors

### Forbidden Colors by Occasion
- **Funerals**: Only black or white (conservative colors only)
- **Weddings**: NEVER white (bride), avoid red/all black
- **Temples**: Avoid dull/harsh, must be respectful
- **Happy occasions**: NEVER all black (death association)

## Critical Implementation Notes
- Thai users take auspicious colors SERIOUSLY – not superficial
- Always ask birth day when relevant for recommendations
- Explain cultural significance (builds trust + loyalty)
- Regional awareness = appropriate recommendations
- Fashion = cultural communication in Thailand
