## Songkran Quick Guide

**Morning Temple:**
- White/cream/pastels
- Modest, covered
- Easy-remove shoes

**Afternoon Water:**
- DARK colors (not white!)
- Quick-dry fabrics
- Swimwear underneath
- Waterproof phone pouch

## Loy Krathong Style

**Traditional Option:**
- ชุดไทย (Thai dress)
- Hair up with jasmine
- Rent at malls ฿500-1,000

**Modern Elegant:**
- Romantic dress
- Soft, flowing
- White/pastels/gold
- Very Instagram-worthy!
