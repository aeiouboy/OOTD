# Summary: Body Types & Styling Guide
**Quick Reference Guide (1-page)**

## Overview
Body type classification with targeted styling for Thai proportions. Flattery through fit, not hiding. Confidence-building approach centered on "no bad bodies."

## Five Body Types & Best Styling

### 1. HOURGLASS (นาฬิกาทราย)
**Feature**: Wide shoulders ≈ wide hips, defined waist
✅ Fitted clothes, wrap dresses, belted styles, peplum tops
❌ Boxy, oversized, tent dresses

### 2. PEAR (สามเหลี่ยม)
**Feature**: Hips wider than shoulders, smaller bust
✅ A-line skirts, boat necklines, bright tops, dark bottoms
❌ Skinny jeans, hip-hugging, cropped tops

### 3. APPLE (กลม)
**Feature**: Weight at midsection, smaller legs, less defined waist
✅ Empire waist, V-necks, flowy fabrics, vertical details
❌ Tight waistbands, crop tops, clingy fabrics, belts at waist

### 4. RECTANGLE (สี่เหลี่ยม)
**Feature**: Shoulders ≈ waist ≈ hips, straight silhouette
✅ Belts, ruffles, peplum, gathered waists, color blocking
❌ Straight unbelted dresses, shapeless cuts

### 5. INVERTED TRIANGLE (สามเหลี่ยมคว่ำ)
**Feature**: Broad shoulders, narrow hips, small bottom
✅ A-line skirts, wide-leg pants, bright bottoms, horizontal stripes on bottom
❌ Shoulder pads, boat necks, sleeveless, dark bottoms only

## Thai-Specific Considerations
- **Average height**: 155-160cm (petite by international standards)
- **Common body type**: Pear or rectangle
- **Common issue**: Long torso + short legs OR short torso + long legs
- **Solution**: Petite sizing + tailoring (affordable in Thailand!)

## Styling for Petite Body (155-160cm)
### GOLDEN RULES
✅ **High-waist EVERYTHING** (lengthens leg = magic!)
✅ **Cropped jackets** (proportional, not overwhelming)
✅ **V-necks + vertical lines** (elongates body)
✅ **Monochromatic outfits** (one continuous line)
✅ **Nude shoes** (extends leg line)
✅ **Proportional jewelry** (delicate, not chunky)

❌ Too much volume, maxi lengths, oversized everything, ankle straps

## Problem Area Solutions

### Wide Shoulders
✅ V-neck, scoop, U-neck, raglan sleeves, dark colors, vertical details
❌ Boat neck, shoulder pads, horizontal stripes, cap sleeves

### Large Bust
✅ V-neck, wrap tops, structured bras, simple designs, darker tops
❌ High necklines, busy patterns, clingy fabrics, tight fits

### Thick Waist
✅ Empire waist, flowy/draped, longer tops, vertical details, A-line silhouettes
❌ Tight waistbands, horizontal stripes at waist, crop tops, clingy fits

### Wide Hips
✅ A-line skirts, dark bottoms, structured fabrics, longer tops
❌ Skinny jeans, hip-hugging, pocket details, light tight pants

### Short Legs
✅ High-waist, nude shoes, monochromatic, pointed-toe shoes, longer tops
❌ Ankle straps (WORST!), capri length, contrasting shoes/pants, heavy boots

## Critical Implementation Notes
- **Proportion > size**: Where clothes HIT more important than size number
- **No "bad" bodies**: Every shape has beautiful solutions
- **Petite = majority of users**: High-waist truly transformative
- **Educate the "why"**: Builds confidence + loyalty
- **Flattery through fit**: Right cut > expensive brand
- **Tailoring mindset**: Affordable in Thailand = always option

## Quick Reference Formula
```
IDENTIFY: Body type + problem areas + height
RECOMMEND: Cuts/styles that balance + flatter + build confidence
```
