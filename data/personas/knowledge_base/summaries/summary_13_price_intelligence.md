## Cost-Per-Wear Formula

```
CPW = Purchase Price ÷ Expected Wears

< ฿50/wear  = Excellent "คุ้มมาก!"
฿50-100/wear = Very Good "คุ้มค่า"
฿100-200/wear = Good "โอเค"
฿200-500/wear = Questionable "คิดดูนะ"
> ฿500/wear = Poor "แพงไป"
```

**Example:**
- ฿3,500 blazer ÷ 300 wears = ฿11.67/wear ✅ Excellent!
- ฿5,000 party dress ÷ 5 wears = ฿1,000/wear ❌ Poor

## When to Buy Full Price vs. Wait

**Buy Now:**
- Need urgently (interview tomorrow)
- Perfect fit, might sell out
- Classic piece for life

**Wait for Sale:**
- Trendy items (ALWAYS wait)
- End of season (clearance coming)
- Can wait 2-4 weeks
