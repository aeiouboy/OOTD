## Key Rules

### Anchor Piece Theory
- Every outfit needs **exactly 1 anchor**
- Anchor = sets tone, most visual impact
- Supporting pieces complement, never compete

### Statement Piece Limits
- **Max 1 statement piece per outfit**
- If statement is anchor → all others must be neutral
- Two statements = visual chaos

### Color Rules
- **Max 3 colors** per outfit
- 60-30-10 ratio: Dominant-Secondary-Accent
- Neutrals: Black, white, navy, gray, beige

## Thai Climate Layering

```
Outdoor: 30-35°C (hot)
Indoor AC: 20-24°C (cold!)

Solution: Always carry removable layer
- Light cardigan for office
- Shawl for versatility
- Light blazer for business
```
