# Summary: Fashion Fundamentals
**Quick Reference Guide (1-page)**

## Overview
Essential fashion fundamentals for daily outfit recommendations: color theory, fabrics, fit, weather, and style categories tailored for Thai climate and preferences.

## Key Points

### Color Theory (Critical!)
- **Basic combinations**: Monochromatic, analogous, complementary all work
- **Thai auspicious colors**: Birth day colors for luck + specific fortune colors (financial, love, career, health, windfall)
- **Kaala-Keenee**: Time-based lucky colors (night/morning/afternoon/evening each day differs)
- **Personal color analysis**: Warm/cool undertones; 4 seasons (Spring/Summer/Autumn/Winter)

### Fabrics & Thai Climate
- **Best for heat**: Cotton, linen, modal, technical (moisture-wicking)
- **Avoid**: Heavy polyester, thick/non-breathable synthetics
- **Thai reality**: AC everywhere (18-20°C indoors) = need layers year-round
- **Rainy season**: Quick-dry fabrics, dark colors, water-resistant shoes

### Fit & Proportions
- **Perfect fit checklist**: Shoulders at seams, no pulling/gaping, sleeves proper length, covers sitting position
- **Thai petite (155-160cm)**: High-waist = magic! Tailoring affordable (฿200-500)
- **Proportion tips**: Monochromatic for height, V-necks elongate, high-waist lengthens legs

### Weather Strategies
- **Hot season (Mar-May)**: Light colors, breathable fabrics, cardigan for AC
- **Rainy season (May-Oct)**: Dark colors, above-ankle lengths, waterproof shoes
- **Cool season (Nov-Feb)**: Layers work! Light jackets, denim perfectly normal
- **AC survival**: Always carry cardigan/scarf (malls freezing!)

### Style Categories
- **Major styles**: Classic, romantic, minimalist, bohemian, edgy, sporty, street style
- **K-fashion huge in Thailand**: Oversized pieces, neutral palette, layering, affordable
- **2025 trends**: Sustainable fashion, Thai pride/local brands, comfort luxury, bold colors, vintage

## Critical Rules & Warnings
- ⚠️ No "bad" bodies – every shape has styling solutions
- ⚠️ Proportion > size – where clothes HIT matters more than size number
- ⚠️ Thai proportions need consideration – international sizing often too long
- ⚠️ AC culture real – always prepare for extreme temperature differences

## Implementation Priorities
1. Assess user's undertone & seasonal color palette
2. Identify body type for personalized suggestions
3. Always mention fit considerations (tailoring available!)
4. Factor in AC needs with layering recommendations
5. Educate on WHY colors/cuts work (builds confidence)

## Quick Reference Formulas
```
COLOR HARMONY: Avoid dull + harsh together. Safe: neutrals + any color
OUTFIT BALANCE: 70% base neutral + 20% complementary + 10% accent
PERFECT FIT: Seam position, no pulling, proper length for occasion
THAI CLIMATE: Light + breathable + layerable + AC cardigan = success
```
