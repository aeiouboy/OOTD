# Summary: Occasions & Dress Codes Guide
**Quick Reference Guide (1-page)**

## Overview
Complete Thai + international dress codes for work, social events, and shopping. Context is everything; same outfit ≠ appropriate everywhere in Thailand.

## Thai Work Environment Dress Codes

### Corporate/Formal (Banking, Law, Government)
✅ Navy/gray/black suit, dress shirt, dress tie, closed-toe shoes, conservative
❌ Bright colors, patterns, casual anything
**Key**: Over-dressed > under-dressed; cleanliness paramount

### Business Casual (Tech, Creative, Startups)
✅ Collared shirt, chinos/dress pants, no tie, professional shoes, polished
❌ T-shirts, denim, sneakers, casual tees

### Smart Casual (Agencies, Media, Retail)
✅ Nice top + jeans/casual pants, casual dress, flats/sneakers, clean appearance
❌ Graphic tees, worn clothing, beachwear

### Casual (Cafes, Studios, Remote)
✅ Clean, presentable, your style shows
❌ Too revealing, very worn, beachwear

## Thai Social Events & Occasions

### Wedding (Guest)
**FORBIDDEN**: ❌ White (bride), all black (funeral), red
**ENCOURAGED**: ✅ Jewel tones, pastels, Thai silk, gold/silver jewelry
**Best colors**: Emerald, sapphire, lavender, blush, coral
**Style**: Modest but celebratory; day = conservative, evening = more glamorous

### Funeral
**REQUIRED**: Black (preferred) or white, long sleeves/pants, closed shoes, minimal jewelry
**Key**: Respectful, conservative, humble appearance; 3-5 day event with varying formality
**Colors**: ONLY black, white, dark gray, dark blue

### Temple Visit
**REQUIRED**: Shoulders covered, knees covered, no tight/revealing, respectful
**Practical**: Slip-on shoes, bring socks (cold floors!), comfortable sitting
**Best**: White or cream, modest colors, removed shoes inside
**AVOID**: Black (funeral), tank tops, short skirts, beachwear

### House Warming
**Style**: Smart casual, festive but not formal, bright colors (luck!)
**Practical**: Easy-to-remove shoes (floor sitting!), avoid tight clothing
**AVOID**: All black (bad luck), too formal, uncomfortable shoes

### Birthday Party
**Depends**: Venue matters! Restaurant (smart casual), club (party wear), home (casual-dressy)
**Bonus**: Consider birthday person's lucky color = shows celebration!

## International Dress Codes (As in Thailand)

### White Tie/Black Tie (RARE in Thailand)
- Very rare (royal events, state dinners)
- Black tie occasionally (galas, high-end events)
- Women: Evening gown, formal; Men: Tuxedo/dark suit, bow tie

### Cocktail (Sometimes in Bangkok)
- Late afternoon/early evening, semi-formal
- Cocktail dress or elegant separates, heels/dressy flats
- Jewelry + accessories welcome

### Smart Casual (Common)
- Neat blouse + nice jeans, casual dress, clean sneakers, put-together

### Beach Formal (Growing - resort weddings)
- Linen dress, flowy elegant, sandals/barefoot
- Thailand-specific = relaxed but elegant

## Shopping Scenarios

### Mall Shopping
**Thai insight**: Thais DRESS UP to shop! Better service = better presentation
✅ Nice jeans + cute top, casual dress, clean sneakers/loafers
✅ BRING CARDIGAN (malls freezing!)
✅ Walking shoes essential

### Chatuchak Market
✅ Light cotton, comfortable sneakers, hat, loose-fitting (sweat!)
❌ Flip-flops (too much walking), tight (will be sweaty)

### Luxury Shopping (Emporia, Paragon)
✅ Smart casual MINIMUM, good shoes, neat appearance
**Key**: Presentation = service quality; dress well = better attention/discounts!
❌ Too casual, worn clothing, sloppy appearance

## Critical Golden Rules
1. **OBSERVE LOCAL CUSTOMS FIRST** - Watch what others wear
2. **PRESENTATION = RESPECT** - Effort shows respect for occasion/people
3. **COLOR PSYCHOLOGY** - Different colors for different occasions (lucky colors!)
4. **FIT > BRAND** - Perfect fit beats designer label
5. **DETAILS MATTER** - Shoes, accessories, grooming noticed
6. **COMFORT = CONFIDENCE** - Can't look good if uncomfortable
7. **REGIONAL DIFFERENCES** - Bangkok ≠ Provinces in formality expectations
