## Overview

Price intelligence enables the AI to make value-based recommendations that maximize user satisfaction within their budget. This includes understanding cost-per-wear, identifying investment pieces, and timing purchases strategically.

**Key Insight:** The best recommendation isn't always the cheapest—it's the best VALUE for the user's needs.

## 1. Cost-Per-Wear Calculation

### 1.1 The Formula

```
Cost Per Wear (CPW) = Purchase Price ÷ Expected Number of Wears

Lower CPW = Better Value
```

### 1.2 CPW Thresholds

| CPW Range | Value Rating | Recommendation |
|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| **< ฿50/wear** | Excellent | "ถ้าใส่บ่อยแบบนี้คุ้มมากค่ะ!" |
| **฿50-100/wear** | Very Good | "คุ้มค่าเลยนะคะ" |
| **฿100-200/wear** | Good | "โอเคค่ะ พอคุ้ม" |
| **฿200-500/wear** | Questionable | "ลองคิดดูนะคะ ใส่บ่อยมั้ย?" |
| **> ฿500/wear** | Poor | "แพงไปหน่อยถ้าใส่ไม่บ่อย" |

### 1.3 Expected Wears by Category

| Category | Typical Frequency | Expected Wears/Year | Lifespan | Total Expected |
|----------|-------------------|---------------------|----------|----------------|
| **Basic Tee** | 2x/week | 100 | 1-2 years | 100-200 |
| **Work Blouse** | 1x/week | 50 | 2-3 years | 100-150 |
| **Jeans** | 2-3x/week | 120 | 2-3 years | 240-360 |
| **Blazer** | 2x/week | 100 | 3-5 years | 300-500 |
| **Party Dress** | 1x/month | 12 | 2-3 years | 24-36 |
| **Wedding Guest** | 2x/year | 2 | 3+ years | 6-10 |
| **Everyday Shoes** | 3x/week | 150 | 1-2 years | 150-300 |
| **Formal Heels** | 2x/month | 24 | 3-5 years | 72-120 |
| **Everyday Bag** | Daily | 300 | 2-3 years | 600-900 |
| **Evening Clutch** | 1x/month | 12 | 5+ years | 60+ |

### 1.4 CPW Examples

**Good Value Example:**
```
Item: Jaspal Black Blazer
Price: ฿3,500
Expected Wears: 300 (work 2x/week, 3 years)
CPW: ฿3,500 ÷ 300 = ฿11.67/wear

Verdict: EXCELLENT VALUE ✅
```

**Poor Value Example:**
```
Item: Statement Party Dress
Price: ฿5,000
Expected Wears: 5 (one-occasion type)
CPW: ฿5,000 ÷ 5 = ฿1,000/wear

Verdict: POOR VALUE (unless very special occasion)
```

**Value-Conscious Alternative:**
```
Item: Versatile Midi Dress
Price: ฿2,500
Expected Wears: 50 (work + casual + dates)
CPW: ฿2,500 ÷ 50 = ฿50/wear

Verdict: MUCH BETTER VALUE ✅
```

## 2. Investment Piece Criteria

### 2.1 What Makes an Investment Piece

An **Investment Piece** is worth paying more for because it:
- Lasts longer (quality construction)
- Never goes out of style (classic design)
- Gets worn frequently (versatile)
- Elevates other pieces (multiplier effect)

### 2.2 Investment Piece Checklist

| Criteria | Weight | Check |
|----------|--------|-------|
| **Classic Design** | 25% | Will it look dated in 2 years? |
| **Quality Construction** | 25% | Seams, fabric, finishing |
| **Versatility** | 20% | How many outfits/occasions? |
| **Fit Quality** | 15% | Does it fit perfectly? |
| **Color Neutrality** | 15% | Works with wardrobe? |

**Score Calculation:**
```
Investment Score = (Classic × 0.25) + (Quality × 0.25) +
                   (Versatility × 0.20) + (Fit × 0.15) +
                   (Color × 0.15)

Score 80-100: Invest confidently
Score 60-79: Good investment
Score 40-59: Consider carefully
Score < 40: Skip or buy budget version
```

### 2.3 Categories Worth Investing In

| Category | Investment Priority | Why |
|----------|---------------------|-----|
| **Quality Blazer** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Transforms any outfit, lasts years |
| **Good Shoes** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Comfort, durability, visible quality |
| **Quality Bag** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Daily use, first impression |
| **Dark Denim** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Versatile, frequent wear |
| **White Button-Down** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Wardrobe essential |
| **Tailored Pants** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Professional foundation |
| **Quality Knit** | ⭐⭐⭐ | Depends on climate needs |
| **Statement Jewelry** | ⭐⭐⭐ | Can transform outfits |
| **Trendy Tops** | ⭐⭐ | Trends change, buy budget |
| **Party Dress** | ⭐⭐ | Low frequency wear |

### 2.4 Investment vs. Trendy Decision Tree

```
Is this item trendy or classic?
├── TRENDY (will look dated in 1-2 years)
│   ├── How often will you wear it?
│   │   ├── Rarely → Buy budget version or skip
│   │   └── Often → Buy mid-range, accept shorter lifespan
│   └── Can you find similar for less?
│       ├── Yes → Buy cheaper
│       └── No → Proceed with caution
│
└── CLASSIC (timeless design)
    ├── Will you wear it frequently?
    │   ├── Yes → INVEST in quality
    │   └── No → Consider if you need it
    └── Does it fit perfectly?
        ├── Yes → INVEST
        └── No → Find better fit first
```

| **COS** | Season end | Jan, Jul | 30-50% |

### 4.3 Sale Strategy Recommendations

**Best Strategy:**
```
1. Wishlist items you want
2. Wait for sale season (May, Nov, Dec)
3. Buy basics at full price if needed urgently
4. NEVER buy trendy items at full price
5. Check online vs. in-store prices
6. Use membership cards for extra discounts
```

**When to Buy Full Price:**
- Interview/important event tomorrow
- Perfect fit, might sell out
- Classic piece you'll wear forever
- Sale price isn't much different

**Never Pay Full Price:**
- Trendy items (will go on sale)
- End of season (clearance coming)
- If you can wait 2-4 weeks

## 5. Budget Strategy by User Segment

### 5.1 Budget-Conscious (< ฿2,000/month)

**Strategy:** Maximize basics, minimize waste

```
Monthly Allocation:
- 50% Basics (1-2 quality pieces)
- 30% Trendy (cheap options only)
- 20% Accessories/Maintenance

Shopping Rules:
✅ Only buy on sale
✅ Focus on versatile pieces
✅ One in, one out policy
✅ Uniqlo for basics
✅ H&M/CPS for trends
❌ No impulse purchases
❌ No single-occasion items
```

**Recommended Brands:**
- Uniqlo (quality basics)
- H&M (trendy)
- CPS (affordable Thai)
- Shopee/Lazada (basics)

### 5.2 Value-Focused (฿2,000-5,000/month)

**Strategy:** Build quality wardrobe gradually

```
Monthly Allocation:
- 40% Investment pieces
- 35% Quality staples
- 25% Fun/trendy

Shopping Rules:
✅ Invest in key pieces
✅ Wait for sales on wants
✅ Build capsule wardrobe
✅ Quality over quantity
✅ Mix budget + investment
```

**Recommended Brands:**
- Jaspal (quality Thai)
- Zara (trendy)
- COS (minimal)
- Uniqlo (basics)

### 5.3 Quality-First (฿5,000-10,000/month)

**Strategy:** Invest in pieces that last

```
Monthly Allocation:
- 50% Investment pieces
- 30% Quality additions
- 20% Accessories

Shopping Rules:
✅ Buy quality, buy less
✅ Build signature wardrobe
✅ Invest in timeless pieces
✅ Quality shoes + bags
✅ Consider tailoring
```

**Recommended Brands:**
- COS, Kloset (premium)
- Jaspal (quality Thai)
- Selected international
- Consider designer on sale

### 5.4 Luxury Preference (฿10,000+/month)

**Strategy:** Curate exceptional wardrobe

```
Monthly Allocation:
- 60% Investment/designer
- 25% Complementary pieces
- 15% Experimentation

Shopping Rules:
✅ Quality is non-negotiable
✅ Designer pieces on sale
✅ Build signature style
✅ Tailoring essential
✅ Unique pieces welcome
```

**Recommended Brands:**
- Greyhound, VATANIKA (Thai designer)
- COS, & Other Stories (premium)
- International designer (on sale)

## 6. Price-to-Occasion Value Matrix

### 6.1 When High Price is Justified

| Occasion Type | Frequency | Justifiable Price | Reasoning |
|---------------|-----------|-------------------|-----------|
| Daily Work | 250/year | ฿2,500-5,000 | High CPW value |
| Weekly Casual | 50/year | ฿1,000-2,500 | Good CPW |
| Monthly Social | 12/year | ฿1,500-3,000 | Moderate CPW |
| Special Event | 2-4/year | ฿3,000-5,000 | Memory value |
| Once (wedding) | 1 time | ฿2,000-4,000 | Keep low, rent option |

### 6.2 Rental vs. Buy Decision

**Consider Renting When:**
- One-time occasion (wedding guest dress)
- Very specific theme (costume party)
- Designer piece you can't afford
- Uncertain about style

**Rental Options in Bangkok:**
- Dress rental shops (฿500-2,000/occasion)
- Thai traditional dress (฿500-1,000)
- Designer bag rental (฿1,000-5,000/week)

### 7.2 Value Messaging Templates

**Excellent Value:**
```
"ถ้าใส่ไปทำงานสัปดาห์ละ 2 ครั้ง ปีนึงใส่ 100 ครั้ง
฿3,500 ÷ 100 = ฿35/ครั้ง คุ้มมากค่ะ! 💯"
```

**Good Value:**
```
"ราคา ฿2,000 ใส่ได้ทั้งทำงานและเที่ยว
ถ้าใส่เดือนละ 4 ครั้ง ปีนึง = ฿42/ครั้ง โอเคเลยค่ะ ✅"
```

**Questionable Value:**
```
"ชุดนี้สวยมากค่ะ แต่ ฿5,000 ถ้าใส่แค่ 3-4 ครั้ง
อาจจะแพงไปนิดนึง 🤔 อยากดูตัวเลือกอื่นมั้ยคะ?"

```
"แบรนด์นี้จะเซลล์เดือนหน้า (May Mid-Year Sale)
