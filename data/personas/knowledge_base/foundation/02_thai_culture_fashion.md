# Thai Cultural Context & Auspicious Colors for Fashion

## Overview

This foundational knowledge document covers two critical areas for OOTDay's Thai fashion expertise:
1. **Thai Cultural Context** - Understanding how Thai society views fashion, dress codes, and presentation
2. **Thai Auspicious Colors (สีมงคลไทย)** - The complete system of lucky colors based on days, occasions, and luck types

Understanding these elements is essential for providing culturally-sensitive fashion recommendations that resonate with Thai users and respect local customs.

---

## PART 1: Thai Cultural Context

### A. Thai Fashion Values

**PRESENTATION MATTERS:**
- Thais judge by appearance
- "Looking good" shows respect
- Effort in presentation is appreciated
- Cleanliness is essential

**MODESTY VALUED:**
- Cover shoulders (temples, formal events)
- Knee-length minimum (conservative settings)
- Not too tight clothing
- Not too revealing
- Fashion-forward OK in Bangkok (varies by region)

**SOCIAL HIERARCHY:**
- Dress appropriately for status level
- Respect for elders requires modest clothing
- Professional appearance = credibility
- Brand consciousness = status symbol

**COLOR SENSITIVITY:**
- Royal family = yellow (shows respect)
- Funerals = black/white only
- Temples = conservative colors required
- Weddings = festive colors, avoid white (bride's color)

### B. Regional Differences

**BANGKOK:**
- Most fashion-forward city
- International influence strong
- Western trends accepted
- Can wear almost anything trendy
- Strong AC = layering often needed

**CHIANG MAI/NORTH:**
- More casual dress accepted
- Artsy/bohemian style appreciated
- Tourist-friendly environment
- Cooler climate (light jacket in winter!)
- More relaxed dress codes overall

**PHUKET/SOUTH:**
- Beach casual is standard
- Resort wear common
- More revealing clothing OK (beach areas)
- But must cover up in town areas
- Tourist-influenced fashion norms

**ISAAN/NORTHEAST:**
- More traditional dress values
- Conservative clothing expected
- Respect local customs importance
- Functional over fashion approach

**SONGKHLA/DEEP SOUTH:**
- More conservative (Muslim influence)
- Must cover shoulders and knees
- Respect local Muslim culture
- Modest clothing highly appreciated

### C. Thai Fashion Etiquette

**DO:**
✅ Remove shoes (homes, temples, some shops)
✅ Dress up for shopping (receive better service!)
✅ Cover up at temples
✅ Respect dress codes for occasions
✅ Be neat and clean always
✅ Consider occasion appropriateness

**DON'T:**
❌ Wear shoes inside homes
❌ Show too much skin at temples
❌ Wear all black to happy occasions
❌ Wear white to weddings (bride color)
❌ Disrespect by being too casual
❌ Wear revealing clothes in provinces

## PART 2: Thai Auspicious Colors - Complete Guide
## สีมงคลไทย - ความรู้ฉบับสมบูรณ์

### 🎯 Overview - What is "สีมงคล"?

Thai auspicious colors are complex and sophisticated - not just "day colors" but include:
1. **สีประจำวันเกิด** (Birth Day Colors) - Lucky colors based on your birth day
2. **สีนำโชคแต่ละเรื่อง** (Specific Fortune Colors) - Colors for different luck types
3. **สีกาลกีณี** (Kaala-Keenee/Time-based Colors) - Auspicious colors by time of day
4. **สีตามราศี** (Zodiac Colors) - Colors based on zodiac sign
5. **สีตามปีเกิด** (Birth Year Colors) - Colors by birth year
6. **สีห้ามในโอกาสต่างๆ** (Colors to Avoid) - Forbidden colors for occasions

### 🌟 Kaala-Keenee Colors (สีกาลกีณี)

**What is Kaala-Keenee?**
"Kaala-Keenee" (กาลกีณี) means "auspicious time" - a sophisticated system calculating lucky colors by time of day, not just the day itself.

**Daily Time Divisions:**
- **ราตรี (Night): 00:00 - 06:00** - Nighttime, rest, introspection
- **รุ่ง (Morning): 06:00 - 12:00** - Dawn, new beginnings, energy
- **สาย (Midday): 12:00 - 18:00** - Afternoon, action, completion
- **เย็น (Evening): 18:00 - 00:00** - Evening, relationships, closure

Each time period has different lucky colors even on the same day!

**Night (00:00-06:00):**
