  ## Color Palette
  - Primary background: #F5F0EB (cream/beige)
  - Primary accent: #C41E3A (deep crimson/magenta) - for buttons, headers, product cards
  - Secondary text: #4A3728 (dark brown)
  - Card background: #FFFFFF (white)
  - Decorative accent: #B8860B (gold/bronze)

  ## Typography
  - Headers: Bold, sentence case with accent color (#C41E3A) for key words
  - Body text: Clean sans-serif, dark brown (#4A3728)
  - Button text: White on crimson background, bold

  - Style selection cards: White/cream background with #C41E3A border on selection

  ### Selection States
