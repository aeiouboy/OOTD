  ## Target Design Requirements

  ### Layout Structure
  1. Header Section (fixed at top):
     - Title: 'What's your style?' in crimson (#C41E3A) bold
     - Subtitle: 'Choose style that sparks you' in dark gray
     - Selected thumbnails row (60x80px) with pink X remove buttons

  2. Carousel Section (scrollable):
     - Horizontal scrolling carousel showing 10 style cards
     - Cards: ~160px wide with rounded corners and white background
     - Left crimson border accent (4px) when selected
     - Card content: full-height image (220px), style name in crimson ('Name · Description' format), gray description text below

  3. Footer Section (fixed at bottom):
     - Circular crimson arrow button (56px diameter) centered
     - Disabled state: gray when no selection

  ### The 10 Fashion Styles (from specs/fashion-styles-output.json):
  1. Minimal · Timeless - 'Less is more, clean, simple, neutral palette, pieces that last forever'
  2. Luxury · Elegant - Premium sophistication
  3. Eccentric · Creative - 'Highly artistic and individual; uses unique shapes, bold colors, and clashing patterns and playful'
  4. Business · Refined - 'Smart, structured, and professional'
  5. Vanilla · Clean - 'Soft neutrals, fresh, and cozy in light tones'
  6. Sporty · Active - 'Athletic wear, functional, ready for movement and confident'
  7. Edgy · Trendy - 'Bold, modern, and daring; follows the very latest fashion trends'
  8. Bohemian · Natural - 'Relaxed, earthy, featuring flowly silhouettes and natural fabrics'
  9. Classic · Old Money - 'Understated quiet luxury built on timeless, tailored, and preppy pieces'
  10. Mystery Style 🔮 - 'Random style' (last card, special styling)

  ### Interaction Behavior
  - Horizontal swipe/scroll through all 10 cards
  - Tap to select/deselect (multi-select allowed)
  - Selected cards show crimson left border + thumbnail appears in header row
  - Tap X on thumbnail to deselect
  - Arrow button navigates to next step when at least 1 selected

  ### Technical Implementation
  - Use embla-carousel for smooth horizontal scrolling
  - Ensure all 10 styles are scrollable (not just visible ones)
  - Cards should snap to position on scroll
  - Warm beige background (#F5F0E8 or similar)
  - Mobile-first responsive design (375px viewport)
