## 🗾 PART 1: Popular Destinations from Thailand

### **A. JAPAN (ญี่ปุ่น) - #1 Thai Destination!**

**SPRING (March-May): Sakura Season! 🌸**
```
Weather: 10-20°C (cool to mild)
Challenge: Thais not used to cold!

WHAT TO PACK:

Layers Essential:
✅ Light cardigan/sweater
✅ Denim jacket or light blazer
✅ Long-sleeve tops
✅ Jeans/long pants
✅ Light scarf (versatile!)
✅ Comfortable walking shoes (LOTS of walking!)

For Photos:
✅ Pastel colors (match sakura!)
✅ Feminine dresses + tights
✅ Korean-style outfits (trendy in Japan)
✅ Light colors for cherry blossoms

Avoid:
❌ Bangkok summer clothes only
❌ Too heavy (not that cold!)
❌ High heels (cobblestones!)
❌ All white (shows dirt fast)

TOKYO Specific:
- Trendy, fashion-forward OK
- Shibuya/Harajuku = dress up!
- Temples = modest (cover shoulders/knees)
- Onsens = bring indoor clothes

KYOTO Specific:
- More traditional/conservative
- Temple-hopping = comfortable shoes essential
- Kimono rental popular (do it!)
- Modest styling preferred

Pro Tips:
🔸 Japanese sizes SMALL (size up!)
🔸 Uniqlo = affordable, quality layers
🔸 Bring compression socks (lots of walking!)
🔸 Indoor/outdoor transition (remove shoes)
```

**SUMMER (June-August): HOT!**
```

WHAT TO PACK:
✅ Similar to Bangkok summer

❌ Heavy fabrics

## 🧳 PART 2: Packing Strategies

### **A. Mix-and-Match Capsule for Travel**

```
10-PIECE TRAVEL CAPSULE:

COLORS: Pick ONE color scheme:
Option 1: Black + White + Denim + 1 accent
Option 2: Navy + Cream + Khaki + 1 accent
Option 3: Gray + Black + White + 1 accent

THE 10 PIECES:

TOPS (4):
1. White/cream t-shirt
2. Striped top
3. Blouse/nice top
4. Sweater/cardigan

BOTTOMS (3):
5. Jeans (dark, versatile)
6. Black pants (dress up/down)
7. Skirt or shorts (season-dependent)

LAYERS (1):
8. Jacket (blazer, denim, or light coat)

DRESS (1):
9. One dress (day to night)

SHOES (3 pairs):
10a. Sneakers (walking)
10b. Sandals/flats (casual)
10c. Dress shoes (nice restaurants)

THIS MAKES: 20+ outfits!
```

```
WEEKEND (2-3 days):
- 2 bottoms
- 3-4 tops
- 1 dress
- 1 jacket
- 2 pairs shoes
- 1 bag
