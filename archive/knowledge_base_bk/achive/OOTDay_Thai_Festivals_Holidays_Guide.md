## 👑 PART 1: ROYAL & NATIONAL HOLIDAYS

### **A. วันเฉลิมพระชนมพรรษา - Royal Birthdays**

**HM THE KING'S BIRTHDAY (July 28) - วันเฉลิมพระชนมพรรษาพระบาทสมเด็จพระเจ้าอยู่หัว**

```
SIGNIFICANCE:
- National holiday
- Government buildings decorated
- Celebration of monarchy
- Public ceremonies
- VERY respectful dress required

COLOR: YELLOW (Royal color of Monday, King's birth day)
```

**WHAT TO WEAR:**

```
GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES / OFFICIALS:
✅ MUST wear yellow
✅ Official uniform if applicable
✅ Very formal, respectful
✅ Closed-toe shoes
✅ Conservative styling

PRIVATE SECTOR:
✅ Yellow encouraged (show respect)
✅ Smart casual minimum
✅ Respectful appearance
✅ Can wear yellow accessories if not full yellow

GENERAL PUBLIC:
✅ Yellow shirt/blouse recommended
✅ Or any respectful attire
✅ Modest, neat appearance
✅ Avoid: Too casual, revealing, sloppy

YELLOW STYLING OPTIONS:

For Women:
Option 1: Full Yellow Elegant
- Yellow dress (knee-length or longer)
- Gold jewelry
- Nude/beige heels
- Simple, elegant
= Very respectful

Option 2: Yellow Top + Neutral
- Yellow blouse
- Black/navy skirt or pants
- Conservative shoes
- Minimal accessories
= Professional respectful

Option 3: Yellow Accessories (if can't wear full yellow)
- Yellow scarf
- Yellow bag
- Yellow brooch
- Main outfit neutral
= Still shows respect

For Men:
- Yellow polo or dress shirt
- Dark pants (black, navy, khaki)
- Dress shoes or loafers
- Belt
- Conservative, neat

Where to Buy Yellow:
- Most stores stock yellow around this time
- Department stores (Central, Robinson)
- Uniqlo, Jaspal, CPS
- Even street vendors!

Avoid:
❌ Too casual (shorts, tank tops, flip-flops)
❌ Too revealing
❌ Messy, wrinkled
❌ Bright conflicting colors
❌ Disrespectful appearance

## 🎊 PART 2: THAI TRADITIONAL FESTIVALS

### **A. สงกรานต์ - SONGKRAN (Thai New Year) - April 13-15**

```
BIGGEST THAI FESTIVAL!
- Water festival
- New Year celebration
- Family time
- Buddha image bathing
- Temples
- WATER FIGHTS everywhere!

SIGNIFICANCE:
- Cleansing, new beginnings
- Respect for elders
- Buddhist merit-making
- FUN water fights!
```

**WHAT TO WEAR:**

```
DAY 1 (April 13) - วันมหาสงกรานต์:

TEMPLE MORNING:
✅ White or light colors (traditional)
✅ Modest, respectful
✅ Thai clothing optional (beautiful!)
✅ Covered shoulders/knees
✅ Will remove shoes
✅ Comfortable for sitting

Traditional Thai Clothing:
Women:
- ชุดไทย (Thai dress)
- ผ้าถุง + sabai
- Very beautiful, respectful
- Can rent or buy
- Perfect for photos!

Men:
- ชุดไทยชาย
- Less common now
- Nice shirt + pants OK

WATER FIGHT (Afternoon):

⚠️ YOU WILL GET SOAKED!

✅ Clothes that can get wet!
✅ Quick-dry fabrics (synthetic, athletic)
✅ Dark colors (white = see-through!)
✅ Swimwear underneath (smart!)
✅ Secure top (not too loose!)
✅ Shorts or capri OK
✅ Sandals/water shoes (will get wet!)
✅ Waterproof bag/pouch
✅ Tie long hair

Practical Songkran Outfit:
Women:
- Athletic tank/tee (quick-dry)
- Board shorts or athletic shorts
- Swimsuit underneath (bra shows when wet!)
- Sport sandals/Tevas
- Hair tied up
- Minimal makeup (will wash off!)
- Waterproof phone case

Men:
- Quick-dry athletic shirt
- Board shorts
- Sandals
- Secure pockets (or no valuables!)

Popular Songkran Style:
- Bright floral shirts (Hawaiian-style)
- Colorful, fun patterns
- Beach casual
- Tourist-chic
- Comfortable for heat + water

Avoid:
❌ White clothes (see-through when wet!)
❌ Anything delicate/expensive
❌ Heels, leather shoes
❌ Too revealing (still Thai culture!)
❌ Loose dresses (will float up!)
❌ Cotton (stays wet, heavy!)

EVENING (Dinner/Socializing):
✅ Change to dry clothes!
✅ Casual comfortable
✅ Restaurants expect normal dress
✅ Can be cute, fun
✅ Festive colors OK
```

**REGIONAL VARIATIONS:**

```
BANGKOK:
- Khao San Road = MASSIVE water fight
- Silom = Office workers join
- Very touristy, wild
- Expect crowds, alcohol
- More revealing outfits common (tourists)

CHIANG MAI:
- More traditional ceremonies
- Old city moat water fights
- Beautiful Thai costumes
- Mix of traditional + modern
- Respect for tradition maintained

PATTAYA:
- Beach city vibe
- More party atmosphere
- Later dates sometimes
- Beach wear common

ISAAN/PROVINCES:
- Very traditional
- Family-focused
- Temple importance
- Less wild water fights
- Respectful dress expected
```

**SONGKRAN SHOPPING:**

```
Before Songkran:
- Stores stock waterproof pouches
- Quick-dry clothing
- Water guns (fun!)
- Festive wear
- Prices normal

Sales:
- After Songkran (mid-April)
- Good deals on summer clothes
```

### **B. ลอยกระทง - LOY KRATHONG - November (Full Moon)**

```
BEAUTIFUL FESTIVAL!
- Float krathong (decorated rafts)
- Make wishes
- Romantic atmosphere
- Buddha worship
- River/water importance

ATMOSPHERE:
- Evening festival
- Candlelight, magical
- Couples romantic date
- Families together
- Very beautiful, photogenic
```

**WHAT TO WEAR:**

```
STYLE: Traditional Elegant

ENCOURAGED:
✅ Thai traditional dress (ชุดไทย)
✅ Very appropriate!
✅ Beautiful for photos!
✅ Respectful to culture
✅ Many Thais wear traditional

Traditional Thai Dress:
Women - ชุดไทย:
- ผ้าถุง (traditional Thai skirt)
- Sabai (shoulder cloth)
- Hair up with flowers
- Jasmine flowers
- Very elegant
- Can rent at malls (฿500-1,000)

Men - ชุดไทยชาย:
- Less common
- Nice shirt + pants OK
- Some wear traditional

MODERN/CASUAL OPTION:

If not wearing traditional:
✅ Elegant, feminine dress
✅ Modest (water activities!)
✅ Comfortable shoes (walking to water)
✅ Light fabrics (still hot!)
✅ Can get wet at edges!
✅ Romantic, pretty styling

Colors:
✅ White, cream (traditional)
✅ Gold, soft colors
✅ Pastels
✅ Nothing too dark/harsh
✅ Feminine, soft colors

Example Outfits:

Option 1: Traditional (BEST!)
- Rent ชุดไทย
- Hair up with flowers
- Minimal jewelry
- Flat sandals
= Beautiful, respectful, Instagram-perfect!

Option 2: Modern Elegant
- White/cream midi dress
- Flowy, comfortable
- Flat sandals (walking!)
- Simple jewelry
- Flower in hair (optional)
= Pretty, appropriate

Option 3: Smart Casual
- Nice blouse + Thai silk skirt
- Comfortable shoes
- Feminine accessories
= Effort but not full traditional

For Men:
- Nice collared shirt
- Long pants
- Loafers or clean sneakers
- Neat, presentable

WHERE IT HAPPENS:
- Rivers, lakes, parks
- Walking on grass/uneven ground
- Getting close to water
- Stairs, crowded areas

PRACTICAL:
✅ Comfortable shoes (will walk!)
✅ Nothing too long (water!)
✅ Can get a bit wet
✅ Bring shawl (cooler evening)
✅ Small bag (hands free for krathong!)

PHOTO OPPORTUNITIES:
- Lantern release (ประทีป)
- Krathong floating
- Candlelight atmosphere
- Traditional dress perfect!
- Very Instagram-worthy!

AVOID:
❌ Too revealing (religious festival!)
❌ Too casual (shorts, tank)
❌ Heels (uneven ground!)
❌ All black (not festive!)
❌ Beach wear (not appropriate!)
```

```

✅ Covered shoulders

✅ No see-through
✅ Closed-toe shoes (or bare feet inside)

✅ Remove shoes inside
✅ Comfortable for sitting on floor

Women:
Option 1: All White (traditional)
- White blouse/shirt
- White pants or long skirt

- Traditional Thai Buddhist

Option 2: Modest Neutral
- Light neutral colors
- Covered appropriately
- Respectful styling
- Still very acceptable

Younger Generation:

EVENING CANDLE PROCESSION (เวียนเทียน):

✅ Comfortable shoes (will walk!)

✅ Traditional, beautiful

   = Royal color (yellow/blue)
