# Realistic Model Generation Prompt - Model3

## Primary Prompt

Generate a full-body, ultra-photorealistic 3D digital model based on the person shown in **[UPLOADED IMAGE]**.

Transform the uploaded photo into a professional fashion model suitable for high-end lookbook photography and virtual try-on applications. The model should maintain the subject's unique facial features while presenting them in a polished, studio-quality format optimized for fashion visualization.

---

## Subject Characteristics

### Facial Features
* **Preserve from uploaded image**: Exact facial structure, skin tone, eye shape, nose, mouth, and any distinctive features
* **Expression**: Bright, genuine smile - warm, friendly, and highly approachable (cheerful and enthusiastic)
* **Eyes**: Natural, engaging gaze with authentic joy - eyes slightly squinted from genuine smile
* **Skin**: Photorealistic texture with natural pores, subtle variations in tone, healthy radiant glow
* **Makeup**: Natural, minimal makeup look - subtle enhancement with focus on fresh, youthful appearance
* **Facial Shape**: Preserve subject's natural face shape, jawline, cheekbones, and proportions

### Hair Styling
* **Reference Style**: Long, dark brown/black hair with side-swept bangs
* **Details**:
  - Hair parted to the side (left or natural part)
  - Long, straight to slightly wavy texture flowing over shoulder
  - Side-swept bangs framing face naturally
  - Rich, dark brown to black color with natural shine and depth
  - Texture showing individual hair strands for realism
  - Natural movement and flow - not overly styled or stiff
  - Length reaching mid-chest to waist (if full body visible in upload)

### Accessories
* **Glasses**: Clear/transparent frame glasses with modern round or oval shape
* **Style**: Minimalist, contemporary aesthetic - thin acetate or metal frames
* **Fit**: Properly positioned on face, complementing facial features
* **Lens**: Clear lenses with subtle realistic reflection

---

## Body and Pose

### Body Type
* **Proportions**: If full body visible in upload, match exactly; if face-only, generate proportionate slim-average build appropriate to subject's apparent age (early 20s aesthetic)
* **Build**: Healthy, natural appearance - slim to athletic-casual (not overly muscular)
* **Skin Tone**: Consistent with face across all visible areas, even and natural
* **Posture**: Upright, confident yet relaxed and approachable - natural standing pose

### Standing Pose
* **Stance**: Standing naturally with feet hip-width apart, slight weight shift for natural feel
* **Arms**: One hand on hip OR arms hanging naturally by sides with slight bend at elbows
* **Hands**: Relaxed, fingers gently curved naturally (not stiff or posed)
* **Shoulders**: Level and relaxed, slightly back for confident posture
* **Head**: Slight tilt toward camera for friendly, engaging feel
* **Overall Vibe**: Casual confidence - approachable model, not intimidating fashion pose

### Pose Requirements for Virtual Try-On
* Arms positioned to allow clear visibility of torso and garment drape
* Body facing forward (0-10 degree rotation maximum)
* No crossing limbs or obscuring body parts
* Clear silhouette for garment overlay processing
* Natural weight distribution on both feet

## Outfit Specifications

### Denim Jacket/Vest (Primary Outfit)
* **Style**: Sleeveless denim jacket/vest with button-front closure
* **Color**: Medium-wash blue denim with authentic fading and texture
* **Fit**: Fitted but comfortable, cropped length ending at natural waist
* **Details**:
  - Two chest pockets with button flaps
  - Metal or dark buttons down front
  - Classic denim stitching visible
  - Collar with slight fold/casual positioning
  - Distressed/worn-in look with natural fading patterns
* **Material**: Cotton denim with visible weave texture and natural variation in wash

### Bottom (Alternative: Neutral Base)
* **For Virtual Try-On**: High-waisted neutral bottoms (jeans or casual pants)
* **Style**: Clean, simple design that doesn't compete with outfit being tested
* **Fit**: Well-fitted, appropriate length
* **Color**: Black, dark blue, or neutral tone

### Footwear
* **Option A**: Casual sneakers (white or neutral)
* **Option B**: Barefoot for seamless product overlay
* **Details**: If shoes included, clean and simple design, well-maintained appearance

## Environment and Setting

### Background
* **Style**: Professional photography studio seamless backdrop OR subtle outdoor-inspired neutral background
* **Primary Option**: Soft neutral gradient - warm beige to light gray
* **Alternative**: Soft-focus natural background with bokeh effect (maintains subject focus)
* **Color**: Neutral tones that don't compete with outfit colors
* **Texture**: Smooth, minimal grain, professional quality
* **Effect**: Creates depth while remaining non-distracting and putting full focus on model

### Floor/Ground
* **Seamless integration**: Background extends to floor without visible horizon line
* **Surface**: Clean, professional studio floor appearance
* **Shadow**: Soft, subtle shadow beneath model for realistic grounding
* **Reflection**: Minimal to no reflection - maintain clean, modern aesthetic

## Lighting Setup

### Primary Lighting
* **Quality**: Soft, natural-looking studio lighting - bright and even without harsh shadows
* **Direction**: Primary key light from 45-degree angle (front-upper right)
* **Intensity**: Bright, cheerful lighting - well-exposed maintaining all detail
* **Fill Light**: Generous fill from opposite side creating bright, minimal-shadow aesthetic
* **Overall Feel**: Bright, optimistic, natural daylight simulation

### Shadow Details
* **Facial Shadows**: Very minimal - bright, open lighting on face
* **Under Chin**: Extremely soft, barely visible shadow
* **Body Shadows**: Gentle shadows only to define form slightly
* **Ground Shadow**: Soft, diffused shadow beneath feet for grounding
* **Overall Effect**: Bright and airy - fashion catalog standard with cheerful mood

### Highlight Management
* **Skin**: Natural, healthy highlights on forehead, cheekbones, shoulders - radiant glow
* **Hair**: Subtle shine showing hair health and natural luster
* **Fabric**: Realistic material-based highlights (natural sheen on denim)
* **Glasses**: Subtle lens reflection, avoiding complete whiteout
* **No Overexposure**: Maintain detail in all highlight areas while keeping bright aesthetic

## Camera and Composition

### Camera Settings Simulation
* **Angle**: Eye-level to slightly below eye-level, creating approachable perspective
* **Distance**: Full-body shot with model filling 70-80% of frame
* **Focal Length**: Simulates 50-85mm portrait lens (natural perspective, flattering compression)
* **Depth of Field**: Entire subject in sharp focus, background can be slightly softer for separation

### Framing
* **Headroom**: Moderate space above head (5-10% of frame height)
* **Foot Space**: Enough to show full feet with small margin
* **Side Space**: Equal margins on left and right, balanced composition
* **Subject Size**: Model fills majority of frame without feeling cramped
* **Orientation**: 3:4 portrait aspect ratio (vertical)

---

## Technical Specifications

### Resolution and Quality
* **Resolution**: 8K (7680 × 4320 pixels) for maximum detail and flexibility
* **Recommended**: 4K Portrait (2880 × 3840 pixels) for optimal balance of quality and file size
* **Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 portrait orientation (optimal for full-body fashion shots)
* **Color Space**: sRGB for web compatibility, Adobe RGB for print quality
* **Bit Depth**: 16-bit per channel for generation, 8-bit for final delivery
* **File Format**: PNG for lossless quality, WebP for web optimization, TIFF for archival

### Quality Standards
* **Photorealism Level**: Indistinguishable from professional fashion photography
* **Detail Level**: Visible skin texture, individual hair strands, fabric weave, realistic materials
* **Noise**: Clean image with minimal digital noise or artifacts
* **Sharpness**: Critically sharp focus on face, maintained sharpness across entire body
* **Color Accuracy**: Natural, true-to-life colors without oversaturation - bright but realistic

## Special Instructions

### For Face-Only Input Photos
If the uploaded image contains only face/head:
1. Generate proportionate body matching apparent age (early 20s aesthetic) and ethnicity
2. Use subject's visible features to infer appropriate body type (slim to average build)
3. Maintain exact facial likeness while creating realistic full-body extension
4. Ensure neck-to-body transition is seamless and natural
5. Default to height range: 160-170cm (5'3"-5'7") for proportionate, relatable appearance

### For Full-Body Input Photos
If uploaded image shows full body:
1. Preserve subject's actual body proportions and build exactly
2. Maintain height-to-width ratios from source image
3. Match muscle definition and natural body shape
4. Translate outdoor/casual setting to professional studio while keeping authentic body type
5. Remove background distractions while preserving subject integrity

### For Upper-Body/Selfie Input Photos (Like Reference)
If uploaded image is upper-body or selfie style:
1. Maintain exact upper body proportions visible in photo
2. Generate lower body proportions that match upper body build naturally
3. Preserve clothing visible in upper body (like denim jacket)
4. Extend to full-body professional shot while maintaining casual, friendly energy
5. Keep same bright, cheerful mood from original photo

### Age Range Adaptations
* **Teens (15-19)**: Maintain youthful, fresh-faced features with age-appropriate casual styling
* **Young Adults (20-29)**: Primary target - reference image aesthetic, bright and relatable
* **Adults (30-39)**: Slightly more refined styling while maintaining friendly, approachable feel
* **40+**: Mature elegance with warm, confident expression

## Virtual Try-On Optimization

### Garment Overlay Requirements
* **Body Position**: Straight-on or slight angle (maximum 10° rotation) for accurate garment mapping
* **Arm Clearance**: Arms positioned away from torso to define garment boundaries clearly
* **Lighting Consistency**: Even, bright lighting allows realistic shadow rendering for virtual clothes
* **Base Layer**: Neutral or simple outfit (like denim jacket) serves as template for garment replacement
* **High Resolution**: 4K-8K ensures sufficient detail for realistic fabric texture overlay
* **Clear Silhouette**: Well-defined body outline for automatic segmentation

### Processing Considerations
* Model should be easily segmentable from background
* Clean body outline for automatic masking and garment fitting
* Consistent pose allows reuse across multiple outfit variations
* Neutral/simple base outfit doesn't create complex overlay challenges
* Bright, even lighting ensures consistent color and shadow rendering
* Natural, relaxed pose appears authentic with various garment styles
