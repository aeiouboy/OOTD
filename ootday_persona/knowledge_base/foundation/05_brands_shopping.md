## PART 2: Shopping Strategy - Smart Shopping Tactics

### A. Before Shopping - Preparation

**Know Your Measurements:**
1. Chest/bust (widest part)
2. Waist (natural waist)
3. Hip (widest part)
4. Shoulder width
5. Sleeve length
6. Overall height
7. Inseam (pants length)

**Check Your Wardrobe:**
1. What do I already have?
2. What gaps exist?
3. What pieces would I wear most?
4. Avoid buying duplicates

**Set Budget:**
1. How much can I spend?
2. What categories matter most?
3. Prioritize quality vs. quantity

**Make a List:**
1. Needs vs. Wants - separate clearly
2. Priority items first
3. Nice-to-haves second
4. Impulse buys third

**Research Prices:**
1. Check multiple stores
2. Compare online vs. in-store
3. Know what things should cost
4. Identify true bargains

---

### B. While Shopping - Serious Strategy

**RULE 1: TRY EVERYTHING**
- Never assume size
- Never assume fit
- Walk around in it
- Sit down in it
- Check in different lighting

**RULE 2: CHECK FABRIC CONTENT**
- Quality fabrics = longevity
- Check fiber content
- Understand care needs
- Consider care cost

**RULE 3: CHECK CARE INSTRUCTIONS**
- Dry clean only = higher costs
- Hand wash = more work
- Machine wash = convenient
- Shrinkage risk?

**RULE 4: WALK AROUND IN IT**
- Does it restrict movement?
- Is it comfortable for real-life wear?
- Does it ride up/down?
- Does it pull/pucker?

**RULE 5: SIT DOWN IN IT**
- Critical for office wear
- Pants shouldn't gap/pucker
- Skirts shouldn't show too much
- Comfortable for real sitting?

**RULE 6: CHECK IN DIFFERENT LIGHTING**
- Color looks different in changing rooms vs. natural light
- Check in mall lighting + outside
- Color accuracy matters
- What looks good in dressing room?

**RULE 7: ASK "WHERE WILL I WEAR THIS?"**
- Be specific (occasion)
- How often will I wear it?
- Can I combine with current wardrobe?
- Is it practical?

**RULE 8: SLEEP ON BIG PURCHASES**
- If hesitant, don't buy immediately
- Come back tomorrow if still interested
- Impulse = regret
- Quality items will still be there

---

### C. Online Shopping Strategy

**KNOW YOUR SIZE PER BRAND:**
- Size M at Zara ≠ M at Uniqlo ≠ M at Jaspal!
- Keep a personal size chart
- Document what fits where
- Reference before ordering

**READ REVIEWS CAREFULLY:**
1. Look for customer photos (real people, real sizes!)
2. Read negative reviews (actual issues)
3. Look for sizing complaints
4. Check fit descriptions

**CHECK RETURN POLICY:**
- How long can you return?
- Free returns?
- Restocking fees?
- Exchange options?

**SAVE ITEMS, WAIT FOR SALES:**
1. Put items in wishlist
2. Wait for sales/promotions
3. Sign up for newsletters (get alerts)
4. Watch for timing (seasonal sales)

**USE SIZE CHARTS:**
1. Measure garment (flat lay)
2. Compare to your measurements
3. Account for shrinkage
4. Consider intended fit (tight, loose, perfect)

**LOOK AT CUSTOMER PHOTOS:**
1. Real people modeling = honest representation
2. See actual fit, not styled shoot
3. Multiple body types = better info
4. Honest feedback from real buyers

**CHECK SELLER RATINGS:**
1. High ratings = reliable seller
2. Low ratings = potential issues
3. Read comments about fit/quality
4. Check customer service feedback

### CALCULATE COST PER WEAR

**The Formula:**
```
Price ÷ Times Worn = Cost per Wear
```

**Example 1: GOOD VALUE**
- ฿3,000 jeans ÷ 100 times worn = ฿30 per wear ✅
- This is excellent value!

**Example 2: BAD VALUE**
- ฿5,000 dress ÷ 2 times worn = ฿2,500 per wear ❌
- This is terrible value!

**Example 3: INVESTMENT PIECE**
- ฿10,000 coat ÷ 200 times worn = ฿50 per wear ✅
- If worn 4x per month for 4 years = worth it!

**RULE: Cost per wear should be under ฿100**

**How to Think About It:**
- Will I wear this at least 20 times?
- If not, it's probably not worth the cost
- Basic pieces = worn many times
- Special occasion = limited wear = lower price justified
- Investment pieces = must be worn frequently
