## Shopping Strategy

### Before Shopping
1. **Know measurements**: Chest, waist, hip, shoulder, sleeve, inseam
2. **Check wardrobe**: What gaps exist? Avoid duplicates
3. **Set budget**: Prioritize quality vs quantity
4. **Research prices**: Know what things should cost
5. **Make list**: Needs vs wants, priorities first

### While Shopping (CRITICAL!)
✅ **TRY EVERYTHING** (never assume size/fit)
✅ **CHECK FABRIC** (quality = longevity, care cost)
✅ **READ CARE INSTRUCTIONS** (dry clean = higher costs)
✅ **WALK AROUND** (does it restrict movement?)
✅ **SIT DOWN** (critical for office wear!)
✅ **CHECK LIGHTING** (color accuracy matters)
✅ **ASK "WHERE WILL I WEAR THIS?"** (practical use?)
✅ **SLEEP ON BIG PURCHASES** (hesitation = warning!)

### Online Shopping (Sizing Critical!)
⚠️ **DIFFERENT BRANDS = DIFFERENT SIZES!** (M at Zara ≠ M at Uniqlo)
- Know YOUR SIZE per brand
- Read customer reviews (real people photos!)
- Check return policy
- Use size charts
- Look at customer photos vs styled shoots

## Value Assessment Formula

### WORTH THE MONEY
✅ Classic pieces (wear for years)
✅ Quality basics (well-made white shirt)
✅ Good shoes (worn often = quality matters)
✅ Perfect fit (even cheap looks expensive)
✅ Investment pieces (worn 100+ times)

### NOT WORTH IT
❌ Trendy pieces (1-2 season wear)
❌ Uncomfortable (won't wear = waste)
❌ Wrong size (return hassle)
❌ Duplicates (won't wear two same items)
❌ "Someday" pieces (never worn)

### COST PER WEAR CALCULATION
```
Price ÷ Times Worn = Cost per Wear
GOAL: Under ฿100 per wear = good value
Example: ฿3,000 jeans ÷ 100 wears = ฿30/wear ✅
Example: ฿5,000 dress ÷ 2 wears = ฿2,500/wear ❌
```
