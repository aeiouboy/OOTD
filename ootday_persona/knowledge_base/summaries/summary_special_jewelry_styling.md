## 🎯 Key Rules & Techniques

### Metal Mixing Mastery
- **2-Metal Rule:** Stick to 2 metals max (safer)
- **3-Metal Advanced:** Add rose gold as bridge
- **Warm + Cool:** Gold/rose gold = warm, Silver/white gold = cool
- **60-30-10 Rule:** Dominant-secondary-accent metal ratio

### Necklace Layering Formula
```
Shortest: 14-16" (choker)
Middle: 18-20" (princess)
Longest: 24-28" (matinee)
Gap: 2-4" between each
Chains: Vary thickness
```

### Ring Stacking Strategy
- **Odd Numbers:** 3, 5, 7 rings look best
- **Balance:** Distribute across both hands
- **Mix:** Bands + statement + midi rings
- **Thai Style:** Right hand for fashion, left for meaningful

## 💎 Thai Cultural Considerations

### Temple & Religious Sites
- **Remove:** All jewelry except wedding rings
- **Avoid:** Ankle jewelry (disrespectful)
- **Buddha:** Never wear as fashion

### Thai Office Culture
- **Conservative:** Minimal, classic pieces
- **Status:** Quality over quantity
- **Gold:** Shows prosperity (appropriate)
- **Diamonds:** Save for senior positions

### Thai Weddings
- **Guest:** Gold shows respect
- **Avoid:** White gold/pearls (bride's domain)
- **Traditional:** Thai gold designs welcomed

## 🌡️ Climate Adaptations

### Bangkok Heat (30-35°C)
- **Avoid:** Tight chokers (uncomfortable)
- **Choose:** Lightweight chains
- **Material:** Avoid leather, fabric
- **Storage:** AC room prevents tarnishing

### Rainy Season
- **Protect:** Silver from humidity
- **Avoid:** Fabric/leather pieces
- **Choose:** Stainless steel, gold
