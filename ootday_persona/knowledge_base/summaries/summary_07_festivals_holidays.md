# Summary: Festivals & Holidays Fashion Guide
**Quick Reference Guide (1-page)**

## Overview
Complete international + Thai festival dress codes. Essential for Thai travelers + core market expertise. Festivals = major engagement opportunity.

## International Festivals (Travel Scenarios)

### CHRISTMAS (Dec 25)
**Europe**: COLD! -5-10°C, heavy coat + layers + gloves + thermal underwear
**Colors**: Red, green, burgundy, cream, gold (festive!)
**Outdoors (Markets)**: Warmth > fashion! Puffer coat, beanie, hand warmers essential
**Dinner**: Smart casual/formal, festive but professional, heels acceptable indoors
**Church**: Conservative (covered), modest length, respectful

### HALLOWEEN (Oct 31)
**Costume culture**: Americans LOVE this! Costume REQUIRED at parties
**Modest ideas**: Witch, Disney character, Harry Potter, food costumes
**AVOID**: Cultural appropriation (geisha, Native American), blackface, offensive costumes
**Weather**: Cool 10-20°C – layer under costume!

### THANKSGIVING (4th Thursday Nov)
**Dress code**: Smart casual to business casual, family gathering
**Colors**: Fall (burgundy, rust, brown, cream)
**Style**: Sweater dress perfect, cozy but polished
**For women**: Nice dress or blouse + pants, comfortable heels

### NEW YEAR'S EVE (Dec 31)
**Times Square**: FREEZING (-5-5°C), stand outside HOURS! Thermal + everything!
**Paris/Europe**: 0-10°C, elegant but warm, can dress up under coat
**Sydney**: SUMMER! 25-30°C, sparkly dress, sandals, beach vibes
**Las Vegas**: DAY casual + NIGHT dressy + heels for clubs
**Tokyo**: Cold 0-10°C, trendy young style, temple visits in traditional dress

### COACHELLA & MUSIC FESTIVALS (April)
**Desert**: HOT days 25-35°C, cool nights 10-15°C
**Style**: Boho chic, crop tops, flowy kimonos, flower crowns, body glitter
**Practical**: Comfortable shoes (standing/walking!), layers for night, sun protection

## Thai Festivals (CORE MARKET!)

### สงกรานต์ SONGKRAN (Apr 13-15) - Thai New Year, BIGGEST!
**MORNING (Temple)**: ✅ White/light colors (traditional, respectful)
- Thai traditional dress (ชุดไทย) optional but beautiful
- ✅ Modest, covered

**AFTERNOON (Water Fights)**: ⚠️ YOU WILL GET SOAKED!
✅ Quick-dry fabrics, dark colors (white = see-through!), swimwear underneath
✅ Secure top (not loose!), sandals, waterproof bag
❌ Anything delicate, heels, expensive, too revealing
**Popular style**: Bright floral shirts, beach casual, fun patterns

### ลอยกระทง LOY KRATHONG (Nov, full moon) - BEAUTIFUL!
**Atmosphere**: Evening candlelight, magical, romantic, photogenic
**ENCOURAGED**: Thai traditional dress (ชุดไทย) – respectful + beautiful
- ผ้าถุง + sabai, hair with flowers, jasmine = elegant
**MODERN OPTION**: Feminine dress, modest, comfortable shoes (walking to water)
**Colors**: White, cream, gold, pastels, soft/feminine colors
**PRACTICAL**: Comfortable shoes, can get wet at edges, bring shawl (cool evening)

### Buddhist Holy Days (Visakha Bucha, Asalha Bucha, Loy Krathong)
**COLOR**: White traditional (purity)
**EVENING CANDLE PROCESSION**: Walking ceremony, holding candles/incense/lotus
✅ White/modest colors, long skirt/pants (sitting possible), covered shoulders/knees
❌ All black (funeral association), revealing, tight

### ตรุษจีน CHINESE NEW YEAR (Jan/Feb)
**COLOR**: RED! (LUCK!) + Gold (prosperity!)
**YAOWARAT (Chinatown)**: Street festival, dragon dances, VERY CROWDED
✅ Red outfit, gold jewelry, festive, comfortable shoes (essential!)
✅ Small crossbody bag (hands-free!), layers (hot + AC)
❌ Black, white, blue (unlucky!), heels (impossible!), large bags

### เทศกาลกินเจ VEGETARIAN FESTIVAL (Sep/Oct)
**COLOR**: White (purity, cleanliness)
**Bangkok**: Moderate observance, white encouraged, simple styling
**Phuket**: EXTREME rituals (body piercings, fire walking, trances) – not for faint of heart!

## Thai Royal & National Holidays (RESPECT CRITICAL!)

### HM KING'S BIRTHDAY (Jul 28)
**COLOR**: YELLOW (respect for monarchy!)
**Government employees**: MUST wear yellow, very formal
**Private sector**: Yellow encouraged, smart casual minimum
**General public**: Yellow shirt/outfit recommended or yellow accessories
**Options**: Full yellow dress (elegant), yellow top + neutral (professional), yellow accessories

### HM QUEEN MOTHER'S BIRTHDAY (Aug 12) - Mother's Day
**COLOR**: Light blue/turquoise
**BONUS**: Wear jasmine flower (traditional, mother's love symbol)
**Style**: Feminine, pretty, family photo-appropriate

### HM KING BHUMIBOL MEMORIAL (Oct 13)
**COLOR**: BLACK (mourning, respect)
**Style**: Very respectful, conservative, closed-toe shoes, minimal jewelry, solemn

### HM KING BHUMIBOL'S BIRTHDAY (Dec 5) - Father's Day
**COLOR**: YELLOW or cream/beige
**Style**: Neat, respectful, family celebration, show effort for father

## Critical Festival Rules
- **Auspicious colors matter**: Not superstition, cultural respect!
- **Occasion-specific attire**: Same outfit ≠ appropriate everywhere
- **Mix traditional + modern**: Both options always acceptable
- **Regional differences**: Bangkok more flexible than provinces
